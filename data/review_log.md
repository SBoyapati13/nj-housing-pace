# Monthly review log

Hand review of what the pipeline can't check automatically: whether the proxy still measures the real thing, and whether the obligations, the law or the Census source have changed. One dated entry per month, with sources. New entries go at the bottom; existing entries are never edited.

## 2026-10-13 (setup)
- Obligations: DCA October 2024 calculation, pinned. By Feb 28, 2025, 282 towns had accepted DCA numbers and 158 used their own (68 challenged); about 380 towns had compliant plans by Jan 5, 2026 (Fair Share Housing Center). Montvale's number was reduced from 348 to 255 in the dispute process (Apr 23, 2025).
- Litigation: a Montvale-led coalition is challenging P.L.2024, c.2; status to be checked monthly.
- Census BPS: no announced format change found; place files for Jan–Aug 2026 were posted Sep 24, 2026.

## 2026-10-13 (review and correction)
- An adversarial review found errors in v2.2 (see CHANGELOG, "Notice of errors"). An "under revision" notice was posted on the map and README.
- Census BPS reporting coverage for NJ varies sharply by year: the share of permitted units that are Census estimates rather than town reports was 1–2% in 2019, 2021 and 2022, and 24–36% in 2023–2025. Newark and Jersey City reported no months to Census in 2025.
- The state's Construction Reporter counts differ: for 2025, 19,865 units (57 towns missing) against Census's revised 27,661; Newark 4,054 against a Census estimate of 16. Both sources will be shown side by side (v3).
- DCA housing-unit files: monthly files in `{year}monthly/` (through 2024) and `{year}m/` (2025 on), yearly revised files in `{year}yearly/`. The 2025 yearly file was not yet posted.
