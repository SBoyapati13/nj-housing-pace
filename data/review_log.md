# Monthly review log

Hand review of what the pipeline can't check automatically: whether the proxy still measures the real thing, and whether the obligations, the law or the Census source have changed. One dated entry per month, with sources. New entries go at the bottom; existing entries are never edited.

## 2026-10-13 (setup)

By Feb 28, 2025, 282 towns had accepted DCA numbers and 158 used their own (68 challenged); about 380 towns had compliant plans by Jan 5, 2026 (Fair Share Housing Center). Montvale's number was reduced from 348 to 255 in the dispute process (Apr 23, 2025).

## 2026-10-13 (review and correction)
- An adversarial review found errors in v2.2 (see CHANGELOG, "Notice of errors"). An "under revision" notice was posted on the map and README.
- Census BPS reporting coverage for NJ varies sharply by year: the share of permitted units that are Census estimates rather than town reports was 1–2% in 2019, 2021 and 2022, and 24–36% in 2023–2025. Newark and Jersey City reported no months to Census in 2025.
- The state's Construction Reporter counts differ: for 2025, 19,865 units (57 towns missing) against Census's revised 27,661; Newark 4,054 against a Census estimate of 16. Both sources will be shown side by side (v3).
- DCA housing-unit files: monthly files in `{year}monthly/` (through 2024) and `{year}m/` (2025 on), yearly revised files in `{year}yearly/`. The 2025 yearly file was not yet posted.
- Correction to the entry above: Newark did report to Census. Its 2025 revised annual figure is 2,190 homes, fully reported (12 months), against 4,054 in the state's data. The "no months reported, estimate of 16" figure belonged to East Newark (a name lookup matched it first). Jersey City's entry is correct: 0 months reported to Census in 2024 and 2025 (Census estimates 2,601 and 2,098), and "Missing Data" in the state's 2025 file.
- Correction to the setup entry: Montvale's Fourth Round prospective need was fixed at 205 by a Superior Court order effective June 12, 2025 (BER-L-750-25). The 255 figure was the April 2025 dispute-program recommendation, not the final number. Added to `data/raw/obligation_final.csv` with a quote from the order.
- Final-obligation records now carry an `evidence` column. Except for Montvale (the order itself) and Roxbury and West Windsor (their adopting resolutions), the "court order" and "settlement" statuses come from town documents or news reports that describe the order or settlement; the orders and settlements themselves have not been read.
- v3 was checked by an independent reviewer that recomputed every town from the raw files: the arithmetic matched, but three rule problems were found (different windows for the two sources, state "Missing Data" processing dates ignored for permits, unconfirmed state zeros). These were registered as v3.1 after seeing v3 results and before computing v3.1 (see CHANGELOG).
- Correction to the entry above: the share of Census figures that were estimates was 0.7% in 2019 (not 1–2%); it was about 2% in 2021 and 2022, and 14–38% in 2015, 2016 and 2020.
- v3.1 was checked by a second independent reviewer that recomputed all 564 towns from the raw files with its own parser: every class, window figure and summary count matched. Its remaining points were text and disclosure fixes (one-source share, too-early period, Census-only figures with no reports, Linden, the 60/62 town-year count, the state's 2025 total), made before publication. The "under revision" notice was then removed.
- 2026-10-20: NJ DCA Division of Codes and Standards replied to the outreach email. It does not send Construction Reporter data to the Census Bureau and believes towns report to Census separately; it noted the three observations for its planned changes and did not dispute them. The data-quality note was updated to say the two counts rest on separate reports from the same towns, and its Census recommendation now asks how towns' reports reach the Survey.
- 2026-10-20: The Census Bureau (Residential Construction Branch) replied to the outreach email. Some New Jersey places report to Census directly; for places that don't, Census receives a monthly data file from DCA. A place with no recent months reported is imputed from the last report received from it. This conflicts with DCA's reply the same day (that it does not send its data to Census). The README and the data-quality note now give both answers and say the connection is unresolved; the note's Census recommendation now asks which places' figures come from the state's file and how those months are marked. Not yet known: whether months taken from DCA's file count as "months reported". A follow-up question to Census is pending.
